Embodied Social-Emotional Education

Training for educators

For educators and schools looking to bring trauma-informed social-emotional learning into the classroom. I offer trainings that help teachers create learning environments that support healthy relationships, access and inclusion, and student agency. I cover topics such as nervous-system regulation, responsible use of power, consent, and boundaries.

Public workshops

For people who want practical, embodied skills for navigating relationships with more empathy, self-advocacy, and care. I offer interactive workshops on listening to your body, making authentic agreements, reparing after mistakes, and cultivating self-compassion.
