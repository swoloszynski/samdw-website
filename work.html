Ways to work with me

Independent Consulting for Organizations

AI product operations consulting

For small and midsize tech companies figuring out how AI fits into their products, workflows, or internal operations. I offer product strategy, workflow and systems design, research synthesis, prototyping, and cross-functional facilitation to turn scattered experiments into something more coherent and useful.

Strategy facilitation

For organizations at moments when the direction feels unclear, priorities are competing, or a group needs help thinking together. I design and facilitate strategy sessions, retreats, stakeholder processes, and decision-making workshops that create clarity and momentum.

Embodied Social-Emotional Education

Training for educators

For educators and schools looking to bring trauma-informed, liberatory social-emotional learning into the classroom. I offer trainings on nervous-system literacy, relational agency, consent and boundaries, power, choice, and creating learning environments that support connection and dignity.

Public workshops

For people who want practical, embodied skills for navigating relationships with more agency, care, and awareness of power. Through Nourished Heart Collective, I offer experiential workshops on consent, boundaries, shame, accountability, nervous-system awareness, and liberatory relationship practices.

With Community Organizations

Art & dance classes

For people around Atlanta who want playful, social ways to make things, move, and connect with others. I teach art classes and partner dance classes that welcome beginners and center curiosity, consent, and accessible participation.
